# Removed bridge and external transport APIs

This fork embeds a Tor client for wallets that connect through public Tor relays.
It no longer configures censorship-bypass bridges, runs external transport
processes, or connects to those bridges through managed or unmanaged pluggable
transports. Direct Tor bootstrap, authenticated relay validation, exit streams,
onion-service clients, and the `keymgr` client-authentication opt-in remain.
Ordinary outbound SOCKS and HTTP CONNECT proxy configuration remains.

## Configuration boundary

`TorClientConfigBuilder` now uses Serde `deny_unknown_fields` at its top level.
Legacy `[bridges]` settings and any other unknown top-level key fail to
deserialize before bootstrap; they cannot silently fall back to direct Tor.
Nested builder validators and the normal typed `from_directories` builder
continue to work. Existing generic unknown-field storage keeps old bridge guard
sets opaque and separate from the active public-relay samples. The historical
unused SQLite bridge table stays in its original migration to preserve cache
schema compatibility.

## Feature and entry-point removals

- Delete the `tor-ptmgr` package, including its IPC parser, process launcher,
  monitor, configuration, and all features: `default`, `full`, `managed-pts`,
  `tor-channel-factory`, `experimental`, `experimental-api`, and
  `__is_experimental`.
- Delete `arti-client::{bridge-client, pt-client}`,
  `tor-guardmgr::{bridge-client, pt-client}`, `tor-dirmgr::bridge-client`, and
  `tor-{linkspec, chanmgr, proto}::pt-client`. Stable `full` selectors stop
  forwarding these features.
- Delete `arti_client::config::{BridgesConfig, BridgesConfigBuilder, BridgeList,
  TransportConfigListBuilder, pt}` and their generated methods; remove the
  bridge/PT re-exports and `TorClientConfigBuilder::bridges`.
- Delete `tor_guardmgr::bridge` (including its feature-off stub),
  `GuardMgr::install_bridge_desc_provider`, and
  `FirstHop::{is_bridge, as_circ_target}`. `GuardMgrConfig` loses the
  `AsRef<[BridgeConfig]>` supertrait and `bridges`/`bridges_enabled` methods.
  The testing `TestConfig` loses its public `bridges` field.
- Delete `tor_dirmgr::bridgedesc` and bridge cache/provider traits and methods.
- Delete `tor_linkspec::{BridgeAddr, BridgeAddrError, TransportId,
  TransportIdError, PtTransportName, PtTargetAddr, PtTargetSettings, PtTarget,
  PtTargetInvalidSetting}`, `ChannelMethod::Pluggable`, and
  `ChannelMethod::{target_addr, transport_id}`. Retain direct channel methods.
- Delete `tor_chanmgr::factory::{AbstractPtMgr, AbstractPtError}`,
  `ChanMgr::set_pt_mgr`, `ExternalProxyPlugin`, and `settings_to_protocol`.
  Delete `tor_chanmgr::Error::{Pt, NoSuchTransport}` and
  `ProxyError::{InvalidSocksAddr, UnrecognizedAddr}`.
- Delete `tor_proto::peer::PeerAddr::Pt`, its `From<PtTarget>` implementation,
  and `From<&PeerAddr> for Option<BridgeAddr>`.
- Remove `AsRef<[BridgeConfig]>` from `TorClientConfig` and both exposed testing
  configurations, plus `AsRef<BridgesConfig>` from `TorClientConfig`. All
  conversions and trait implementations belonging to deleted types disappear.
- Delete `ErrorDetail::{BridgeDescMgrSetup, PluggableTransport}` from
  `arti-client`; those variants were internal normally and public under the
  `error_detail` opt-in.

The removed code also removes direct `tor-guardmgr` edges to `dyn-clone` and
unused `base64ct`, its bridge-only `tor-protover` edge, and `tor-linkspec`'s
last `serde_with` edge. Other retained packages still use those registry crates.
